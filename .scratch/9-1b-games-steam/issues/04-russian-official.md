## What to do

- **`russian` (pure).** `Support(text, voice)` and «no data»; the official look and its strict reading from a snapshot; the fan part is passed in (ticket 05 fills it: for now it is always «no data»); `line(...)` with the rows of 5.2 — «текст и озвучка — официально», «текст — официально; озвучка — народная» and so on, «нет», «официально нет», «нет данных», the «(ПК)» rule (`GameScope.told` minus PC is not empty and the body rests on PC-only data; never after «нет данных»); «what appeared» between two looks (a part that is yes now and was not; no data counts as no); a source that once had data and later says nothing keeps the previous value.
- **IGDB side.** The own query (`GAME_QUERY`) also asks `language_supports.language,language_supports.language_support_type`; `GameState.russian` is `{"text": bool, "voice": bool}` or `null` (IGDB has no languages), no key — not asked yet, read strictly. Russian is language 22: text = type 2 or 3, voice = type 1; a non-empty list without 22 → both no. Comes with adding and every own poll, no extra request.
- **`sources/steam_languages` and the client.** `SteamProvider.store_items` gets a languages request (`"data_request": {"include_supported_languages": true}` only, country `US` whatever the store country, packs of 100). Russian is `elanguage` 8: text = `supported` or `subtitles`, voice = `full_audio`; a non-empty list without 8 → Support(no, no); a missing or empty list, or `success` 15 → no data; a list that is not records with yes/no flags → the answer about that game is unreadable (a failure of that game); a failed request → every game of the pack fails.
- **`pollers/game_russian` (5.5).** At the end of each pass after prices and follow-ups (`GamePoller.end_of_pass`): `for_russian` (status «Хочу» or «В процессе», any platforms) in one place; the reconciliation makes an empty snapshot `russian_official` (due now) for such a game without one and never drops one. Due: games for Russian whose time has come, `next_check_at` empty first. Asks Steam for games whose own state has a Steam number (the same number on two games asked once), else uses IGDB. The look: `{"source": "steam", "appid": "…", "text": …, "voice": …}`, `{"source": "igdb", "text": …, "voice": …}`, `{"source": null}`; while Steam has none and IGDB is «not asked yet» nothing is stored. Reference point (no events): the snapshot is empty; the source or the Steam number changed; the first check after «Вернуть в список» (`observed_at` earlier than `restored_at`). Otherwise each part that appeared → one event. Events of all games of the check queue in one write session through `EventQueue.enqueue_all` with `DueTitle.statuses` = («Хочу», «В процессе»); then each look is stored; next check in 6 hours, after a failure in 30 minutes; a failed Steam request reports «could not check» with the source «Steam» (`failures.source_name` maps `russian_official` to «Steam»); a game whose own state cannot be read is skipped with one log line a day.
- **`DueTitle.statuses: tuple[TitleStatus, ...] | None = None` (3.2).** When set, `still_checked` also requires the title's status to be one of them.
- **The Russian part of `after_marks` (3.1).** The undelivered Russian events (`russian_events.TYPES`) of games no longer in «Хочу» or «В процессе» are withdrawn; their snapshots stay.
- **`russian_events` (5.8, official).** `game_russian_official`: texts for text / voice / both («🎮 В «Stray» появился русский текст», «🎮 В «Hollow Knight: Silksong» появилась русская озвучка», «🎮 В «Hades II» появились русский текст и озвучка»), « (ПК)» by the rule of 5.2, the key `moved_key("game:<id>:russian:official:<parts>", previous_look)` (parts `text`, `voice`, `text_voice`), the payload `{"text", "name", "parts", "source", "steam_id", "pc"}`, buttons «📄 Карточка» and «🛒 Открыть в Steam» when the payload has `steam_id` (`actions.event_actions` reads `russian_events.TYPES`, the one list), the sample «Игра: появился русский язык» («Hollow Knight: Silksong», voice, `steam_id` 1030300) among «Тестовое событие».
- **The cards.** `CardPart.russian: RussianView | None` (the line, the hints, the form flag — here always hidden). Web block «Русский язык» after «Платформы», before «Steam»: shown for «Хочу» and «В процессе», for another status only when it has data; the line «**Русский:** …», the hints in the order of 5.3 that apply without ZoG (sources «Steam — проверено сегодня в 09:00» or «IGDB — …» through `format_past_moment`, nothing checked yet «Ещё не проверяли — строка появится через минуту-другую», the «(ПК)» hint, «нет данных» after a check — with and without a Steam page, the other-status hint). The bot card: the same line after «Цена в Steam: …» (or the platform lines), before the store links.
- **Leaving and coming back (5.9).** «Прошёл», «Бросил» stop the checks (the due queries read the status), undelivered Russian events go, snapshots stay; «Вернуть в список» → the next check of each part is the reference point. Adding asks nothing more and cannot fail because of Steam languages: the next pass makes both snapshots due.
- **Docs:** docstrings, `tools\code_map.py`, `docs/tests-map.md`, rule 7 table (new modules and rows for `checks` with `statuses`); `CONTEXT.md` — «Русский язык», «Проверка русского языка» (the ZoG half of the entry is added by ticket 05); `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, «External services»: Steam (languages in `US`), IGDB (`language_supports`).
